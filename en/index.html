Onion Routing Anatomy

Anatomia di Tor — the Tor protocol, byte by byte

A technical book on how Tor works, from the bytes two relays exchange up to design choices, real attacks and open problems. Every mechanism is rebuilt and tested in a laboratory, on a private Tor network that runs on an ordinary computer.

The book is written in Italian. It is free, and its sources — text, figures, code and laboratories — are open to everyone under the GNU GPL.
