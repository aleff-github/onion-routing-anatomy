AI transparency

This page summarises how artificial intelligence was used to make the book, based only on what the project's records document. Where something cannot be verified, it says so.

The book draws on study notes and documents I produced over the years. Under my direction, AI tools substantially expanded, explained, structured and drafted the present work. Despite internal reviews and checks, errors may remain; readers can report and correct them. The guiding principle is "diffusione dei saperi senza fondare poteri" — sharing knowledge without establishing power. It is free through the project's official channels, under GPL-3.0-or-later, which also permits commercial redistribution on its terms.

My part

  • The origin of the project: my study of Tor, begun for my bachelor's thesis and continued afterwards. The thesis is not in the repository, so it is not possible to say which passages derive from it.
  • The direction of the work and its decisions: goals and scope, the model chapter for tone and level, title, license, free distribution, authorisation of each live laboratory run.
  • The review and maintenance of the work, and editorial responsibility for what is published.

What AI tools did

  • The current text, figures, exercises and solutions were drafted from September 2026 in sessions with an AI coding assistant, Claude Code, under my direction. They should be regarded as AI-generated under human direction, not as text I wrote and only polished with AI.
  • The laboratory code, the scripts and the build system were written in the same way.
  • The translations were generated with AI tools; their review was also carried out in sessions with AI tools.
  • The project documentation, including this website, was drafted with AI from information I provided.

How the work is checked, and the limits

Internal review. I review and maintain the work myself, and it keeps being corrected and improved.

Automated checks: live runs of the Italian laboratories on a private Tor network (53/53 and 14/14 checks), offline tests, builds of the PDF and EPUB with programmatic checks and EPUBCheck validation.

Independent review: none so far — no peer review or validation by external experts, no review of the legal chapter by a lawyer, no review of the translations by native speakers.

Errors may therefore remain. If you find one, please report it.
