About the translations

English, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese translations were generated with AI tools from the Italian original. The source files are available in the repository; no translated PDF or EPUB edition has undergone independent review.

Automated checks do not replace native-speaker review or live testing of the translated laboratories. The Italian original remains the reference.

For more information, see docs/translations/. This website is available in five languages.

Help review a translation

If you are a native or fluent speaker, a review of even one chapter makes a difference. You do not need to know LaTeX or Git.

Join the translation review Report a translation problem

The Italian text is the reference: a correction to a translation never changes the Italian original silently. If a problem comes from the original, it is reported and fixed there first.
